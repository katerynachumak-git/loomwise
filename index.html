Is Loomwise a fit?

We work best with companies that are past the hype and ready to change how work gets done.

Great fit

  • B2B SaaS or tech-enabled services, 50–500 people
  • Already paying for AI tools (Copilot, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini…)
  • A leader who owns the outcome — COO, CPO or Head of Ops
  • Teams in product, customer success, support or operations

Probably not

  • You need a custom model trained from scratch
  • You're looking for a one-off keynote on "the future of AI"
  • There's no team ready to own the new workflow
